# Storylines

At 11:52 you ask Magpie and it answers with the source. Then the day is searchable, loose ends become reminders, and it works on your phone. Off-white Magpie UI ground; teal brand world for the reveal and end.

C. "Type carries the argument": giant kinetic type on teal ("You saw it. You forgot it. Magpie didn't."), cutting hard to real UI.
